Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what the GBLiNE TV app (Android TV, Google TV, phones, tablets and web) does with information about you. In short: we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect, sell or share personal data.

1. What stays on your device

The app keeps its settings only in your device's own storage: your language, chosen weather city and temperature unit, favourite channels, the country you last browsed, the playlists you add yourself (including Xtream Codes server, user name and password, if you enter them), cached channel lists and settings, the date the app was first opened (for the 30-day ad-free period) and a counter of viewing time (to show one ad every 20 minutes). None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. What the app downloads, and from whom

To work, the app connects to the services below. Like any internet connection, these services can see your device's IP address and basic technical data (for example the app or browser type). The app sends them no name, e-mail, account, advertising ID or precise location.

  • TV and radio streams are played directly from the broadcasters' own servers and platforms. GBLiNE TV does not host or relay any video or audio.
  • Channel and station lists: the public iptv-org database and Radio Browser.
  • TV guide, app settings and app updates: GitHub (files we publish there).
  • Weather: Open-Meteo, which receives the coordinates of the city you picked (a city, never your GPS position).
  • Ads (after the first 30 days): the ad pictures or videos are loaded from the addresses listed in our settings file. We do not use advertising IDs, tracking or personalised ads.

The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions. Your country is guessed from your device's language settings only.

3. Adding a playlist with your phone (QR code)

If you choose to add your own playlist by scanning a QR code, the playlist address (or Xtream Codes details) you type on your phone is sent over an encrypted connection to our pairing service (hosted on Supabase) under the random code shown on your TV. Your TV collects it and it is deleted at that moment; anything not collected is deleted automatically after 15 minutes. It cannot be read or listed by anyone else.

4. Children

GBLiNE TV is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Security

All connections made by the app itself use HTTPS where the source supports it. Data kept on your device is excluded from device backups.

6. Content and rights holders

GBLiNE TV only lists channels that broadcast freely to the public, never pay-TV, and never changes or hides broadcasters' logos. If you own the rights to a channel and want it removed, contact us (below) and we will remove it from the app, normally within a few days and without needing an app update.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy or a removal request: open an issue at github.com/Adam-CZPG/gbline-data/issues.

Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 28-oktabr

Ushbu siyosat GBLiNE TV ilovasi (Android TV, Google TV, telefon, planshet va web) siz haqingizdagi ma'lumotlar bilan nima qilishini tushuntiradi. Qisqasi: hisob ochish talab qilinmaydi, biz shaxsiy ma'lumot to'plamaymiz, sotmaymiz va hech kimga bermaymiz.

1. Qurilmangizda nima saqlanadi

Ilova sozlamalarini faqat qurilmaning o'z xotirasida saqlaydi: til, ob-havo shahri va harorat birligi, sevimli kanallar, oxirgi ko'rilgan davlat, o'zingiz qo'shgan pleylistlar (Xtream Codes server, login va parol kiritgan bo'lsangiz — ular ham), kanallar ro'yxati va sozlamalar keshi, ilova birinchi ochilgan sana (30 kunlik reklamasiz davr uchun) va ko'rish vaqti hisoblagichi (har 20 daqiqada bitta reklama uchun). Bularning hech biri bizga yuborilmaydi. Ilovani o'chirsangiz yoki ma'lumotlarini tozalasangiz, ular ham o'chadi.

2. Ilova nimani va qayerdan yuklaydi

Ishlashi uchun ilova quyidagi xizmatlarga ulanadi. Har qanday internet ulanishidagidek, ular qurilmangizning IP manzilini va oddiy texnik ma'lumotni (masalan, ilova yoki brauzer turini) ko'rishi mumkin. Ilova ularga ism, e-mail, hisob, reklama ID'si yoki aniq joylashuvni yubormaydi.

  • TV va radio efirlari to'g'ridan-to'g'ri telekanallarning o'z serverlari va platformalaridan ijro etiladi. GBLiNE TV hech qanday video yoki audioni o'zida saqlamaydi va uzatmaydi.
  • Kanallar va radiostansiyalar ro'yxati: ochiq iptv-org bazasi va Radio Browser.
  • Teledastur, ilova sozlamalari va yangilanishlari: GitHub (biz joylagan fayllar).
  • Ob-havo: Open-Meteo — unga siz tanlagan shaharning koordinatalari yuboriladi (shahar, hech qachon GPS joylashuvingiz emas).
  • Reklama (dastlabki 30 kundan keyin): reklama rasmi yoki videosi sozlamalar faylimizdagi manzillardan yuklanadi. Reklama ID'si, kuzatuv yoki shaxsiylashtirilgan reklama ishlatilmaydi.

Ilova joylashuv, kamera, mikrofon, kontaktlar yoki xotiraga ruxsat so'ramaydi. Davlatingiz faqat qurilmaning til sozlamalaridan aniqlanadi.

3. Telefon orqali pleylist qo'shish (QR kod)

QR kodni skanerlab o'z pleylistingizni qo'shsangiz, telefonda kiritgan manzil (yoki Xtream Codes ma'lumotlari) shifrlangan ulanish orqali bizning ulash xizmatimizga (Supabase'da) TV'dagi tasodifiy kod bilan yuboriladi. TV uni oladi va shu zahoti o'chiriladi; olinmaganlari 15 daqiqadan keyin avtomatik o'chadi. Uni boshqa hech kim o'qiy yoki ko'ra olmaydi.

4. Bolalar

GBLiNE TV 13 yoshgacha bo'lgan bolalarga mo'ljallanmagan va biz bolalardan ataylab ma'lumot to'plamaymiz.

5. Xavfsizlik

Ilovaning o'zi qiladigan barcha ulanishlar manba qo'llasa HTTPS orqali bo'ladi. Qurilmadagi ma'lumotlar zaxira nusxasiga (backup) kiritilmaydi.

6. Kontent va huquq egalari

GBLiNE TV faqat hammaga bepul efir qiladigan kanallarni ko'rsatadi, pullik TV'ni hech qachon ko'rsatmaydi va telekanallar logotiplarini o'zgartirmaydi yoki yashirmaydi. Agar siz kanal huquqi egasi bo'lsangiz va uni olib tashlashni istasangiz, quyidagi manzilga yozing — biz uni odatda bir necha kun ichida, ilovani yangilamasdan olib tashlaymiz.

7. O'zgarishlar

Siyosat o'zgarsa, yangi versiyasi yangi sana bilan shu sahifada e'lon qilinadi.

8. Aloqa

Maxfiylik bo'yicha savollar yoki olib tashlash so'rovi: github.com/Adam-CZPG/gbline-data/issues sahifasida murojaat oching.
